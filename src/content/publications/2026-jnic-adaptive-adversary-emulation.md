A solution based on Adversary Emulation (AE) that dynamically adapts attack tactics to the targeted infrastructure, balancing faithful imitation of the emulated adversary with adaptability to the target environment. Guided by Machine Learning and industrial simulation, it enables an AI agent to train efficiently by learning from the environment and its weaknesses, supporting threat hunting and proactive defense evaluation in critical infrastructures and IIoT settings.
