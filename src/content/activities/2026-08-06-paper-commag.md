**Adaptive Adversary Emulation Framework for Cyberwarfare Operations** was published online in *IEEE Communications Magazine* (vol. 64, no. 10, October 2026, pp. 54–59). It introduces OPFOR, a modular adversary emulation framework that combines cyber threat intelligence with knowledge of the network topology to generate infrastructure-aware attack paths, validated with a Stuxnet-inspired operation run by a reinforcement-learning agent on a simulated nuclear plant network. It is joint work with Cristina Alcaraz, Mario Reyes and Javier Lopez.
