**Adaptive Adversary Emulation Applied to Critical Infrastructures and IIoT Networks** was published in the proceedings of the *Jornadas Nacionales de Investigación en Ciberseguridad* (JNIC 2026), held 6–8 May 2026 at the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya in Barcelona. It presents an adversary emulation approach that adapts its attack tactics to the targeted infrastructure, guided by machine learning and industrial simulation so that an AI agent learns from the environment and its weaknesses. It is joint work with Mario Reyes and Javier Lopez.
